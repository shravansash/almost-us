Almost Us
A story of right person, wrong time
Synopsis

Adhil, a young filmmaker torn between memory and motion, is followed in Almost Us as he deals with love, grief, and the silent burden of unresolved feelings. He discovers through two radically different relationships that love is more about how someone profoundly transforms you before they depart than it is about staying. The story follows how miscommunications, timing, and unspoken truths shatter something once lovely against the backdrop of movie sets, long drives, abandoned colleges, and late-night silences. In his quest for closure, Adhil learns that some love stories end in understanding and acceptance, which can still mean everything, rather than in being together.
